Expert:  RAG is a technique in NLP where we use a combination of retrieval and generation to produce more accurate and relevant text. Essentially, we're using a large language model to generate text, but then we're retrieving relevant information from a knowledge base to fine-tune the output. I've applied RAG in several projects, including the job recommendation platform I mentioned earlier. It's been really effective in producing more accurate and relevant job recommendations for users.

KEY TERMS
---------
• AI-powered automation: The use of artificial intelligence to automate business processes and workflows.
• Machine learning: A type of artificial intelligence that enables computers to learn from data and improve their performance over time.
• Retrieval-augmented generation (RAG): A technique in natural language processing that combines retrieval and generation to produce more accurate and relevant text.
• NLP: Natural language processing, a subfield of artificial intelligence that deals with the interaction between computers and humans in natural language.
• Knowledge base: A database or repository of information that can be used to retrieve relevant data and fine-tune the output of a machine learning model.
• CRM: Customer relationship management, a business process that involves managing interactions with customers and clients.
• Recruitment: The process of finding and hiring new employees for a company or organization.
