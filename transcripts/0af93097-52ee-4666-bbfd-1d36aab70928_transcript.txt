Expert: Sure, Alex. LLM APIs refer to Large Language Model Application Programming Interfaces, which enable developers to integrate AI models like language translation, sentiment analysis, or text summarization into their applications. Prompt engineering is the process of designing and optimizing the input text, or prompt, to elicit the desired response from an AI model. RAG, or Retrieval-Augmented Generation, is a technique that combines the strengths of retrieval-based and generative models to produce more accurate and informative responses. NLP stands for Natural Language Processing, which is a subfield of AI that deals with the interaction between computers and humans in natural language. And semantic search is a type of search that focuses on the meaning and context of the search query rather than just the keywords.
