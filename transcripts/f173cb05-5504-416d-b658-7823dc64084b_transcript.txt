KEY TERMS
---------
• AI: Artificial intelligence refers to the development of computer systems that can perform tasks that typically require human intelligence.
• Machine Learning: Machine learning is a subset of AI that involves training algorithms to learn from data and improve their performance over time.
• NLP: Natural Language Processing is a field of study that focuses on the interaction between computers and human language, enabling computers to understand and generate text or speech.
• LLM: Large Language Model is a type of AI model that uses neural networks to process and generate human-like language, often used for tasks such as text completion and generation.
• LSM-tree: LSM-tree is a type of storage engine that uses a log-structured merge approach to store and manage data, providing high performance and reliability.
• Prompt Engineering: Prompt engineering is the process of designing and optimizing input prompts to elicit specific responses from AI models, such as Large Language Models.
• GenAI: General-purpose AI refers to AI systems that can perform a wide range of tasks and can learn and adapt to new situations, rather than being limited to a specific task or domain.
