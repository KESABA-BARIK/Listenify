Host: That makes sense. I've also heard of Listenify, a platform you developed that converts research paper PDFs into podcast-style conversations. Can you walk us through how that works?
Expert: Listenify is a unique project that leverages large language models (LLMs) and edge-based text-to-speech (TTS) synthesis to generate audio conversations from research papers. The idea is to make complex research more accessible and engaging for a broader audience. I used LLM APIs to analyze the text and generate a script, which was then converted into audio using Edge-TTS.

Host: That's impressive, indeed. I've also heard that you're working on a persistent key-value store called shradsDB. Can you tell us more about that project and what makes it unique?
